# Surface brief: site visual world (landing, Mission, Agentic Governance Workflow)

Scope: all three pages share one world. Landing page is Persuade; Mission and the workflow page are Read inside the same world.
Audience and job: funders judging credibility in a few minutes (see PRODUCT.md); action is reading the Mission and getting in touch.
Pinned by the owner: the three-ring logo shape (colour open); rings carry the Organisation / Verification / Regulation split; scale is the idea; scroll and click drive it; the hero hosts the five-measure acceleration chart (data and research live on `feat/acceleration-chart`). Keep the diagrams and the publication browser as content. Anti-reference: the generic AI-safety look.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is one continuous change of scale, after the Eames film Powers of Ten. It opens on the pace of AI development, then each logo ring is a frame that zooms out from inside one organisation, through verification, to regulation. It refuses the category default: centred headline, two buttons, card grid, white with one blue.

OWN-WORLD: Owner-pinned palette: navy ink (#101b37 family) on white reading paper, pale navy-tinted frames for the opening, the zoom and the contact, and one magenta (#e41376 family) for the active frame corners, the active ring and the primary action. Deep cyan only as a diagram role. Schibsted Grotesk for labels, headings and scale captions; Literata for reading; tabular figures. Frame corners and the three tangent rings are the only ornaments. (Replaces the first build's black film frames and frame yellow, which the owner rejected.)

STORY: The visitor sees AI accelerating on five measures, understands governance must keep pace at three levels, sees the evidence from the innermost ring, reads the Mission, gets in touch.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Pale tinted frame. Headline top left with the magenta "Read the mission" action under it; the acceleration chart fills the right ~60% with five measure tabs; magenta frame corners outline the active measure; a scale caption sits bottom left; the rings peek at the bottom edge, announcing the zoom.

FORM: Powers of Ten scale film; rank 1 of the grounded list (pick card); seed key 7fbdb9f5, re-roll 1. Signature interaction: a sticky three-ring zoom section, scroll moves the camera outward ring by ring, a click on a ring jumps to its level; reduced motion shows three static frames.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
