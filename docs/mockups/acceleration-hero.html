AI is developed and deployed faster than governance can follow.

Our argument

Governance has to keep pace

Governance work is still mostly manual: people read the rules, interpret them and check evidence by hand. We argue that AI governance across all levels has to keep up with this pace, and that neglecting it poses a systemic risk.

Scalable Governance is an applied research initiative with the goal of scaling AI governance to the pace of rapidly increasing capability and volume of AI systems, without losing the human judgment and accountability that good governance requires.

We are based at the TUM Professorship of Societal Computing.

What we build

We are building an Agentic Governance Workflow: agent skills that enable effective and accountable human-AI collaboration for governance work. People make the decisions; agents do the repeatable parts.

It rests on a compliance chain. Take one obligation from the EU AI Act: the workflow follows it through five steps and counts it as done only when the AI system itself has changed.

How the workflow works
  1. ObligationWhat the regulation requires
  2. PolicyWhat it means for the organisation
  3. ControlHow to implement the requirement
  4. System impactWhat changes in the AI system
  5. EvidenceProof it was implemented
